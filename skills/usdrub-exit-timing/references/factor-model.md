# Factor model reference

## RUB-supportive channels

- higher CBR real rates
- higher exporter FX sales
- stronger oil price *and* export volume
- lower import demand
- lower Ministry of Finance net FX purchases
- reduced sanctions/payment friction
- improved Russian export logistics
- credible de-escalation reducing infrastructure losses

## RUB-negative channels

- lower Brent/Urals
- lower physical oil/product exports
- refinery/port/pipeline damage
- larger budget deficit / year-end fiscal impulse
- higher Ministry of Finance FX buying
- weaker CBR guidance / rate cuts
- stronger Fed / global USD
- new sanctions on oil buyers, payments, shipping or insurance
- increased import demand
- reduced exporter FX sales

## Timing priority

USD/RUB can move before macro data reaches the budget because markets price expected future flows.

For a 2-5 week exit decision prioritize:

1. actual FX flow
2. scheduled catalysts
3. oil/export shocks
4. rate expectations
5. price-action confirmation

Do not use annual macro forecasts alone to time a short-horizon exit.
